# Mira of the Fens

Mira runs the eel traps south of [[Saltmarsh Landing]] and has done since she was
a girl. She is #reclusive and speaks to almost no one.

> [!secret]
> Mira knows where the old causeway surfaces at low tide. She has never told
> anyone, and will not unless she is convinced the asker means the fens no harm.

She is tall for a fenlander, weathered, and habitually barefoot.

Her aim is simply to be left alone.
