type: character
name: King Yuna
---

**1. History**

Yuna took the salt throne at nineteen after his three older brothers drowned in
the same season. He has ruled for thirty-one years and has never left the coast.

**2. Manner**

Blunt to the point of rudeness. Dislikes ceremony and cuts speeches short.

**3. Appearance**

Heavyset, close-cropped white beard, a burn scar across the right forearm.

**4. What He Wants**

To see the salt tithe abolished before he dies.
