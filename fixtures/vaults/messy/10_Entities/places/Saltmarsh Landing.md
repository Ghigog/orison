## Description

A crooked jetty and eleven houses on stilts. The tide goes out nearly a mile and
comes back faster than a person can walk.

## Smells Like

Rot, tar, and woodsmoke. This heading matches no canonical field and must survive
compilation somewhere retrievable rather than being discarded.
