---
type: lore
name: The Salt Tithe
---

Every household within a day of the water owes one measure of salt a year to the
crown. The tithe predates the current dynasty and nobody can produce the original
grant.

It is collected by the salt guard, resented universally, and defended by
[[The Chancellor]] on the grounds that abolishing it would require admitting it
was never lawful.

The tithe riots began at the granary at [[Saltmarsh Landing]].
