## Biography

Bram Holt has held the toll post at Stonebridge since the old warden died. He is
not a soldier and says so to anyone who calls him one.

## Personality

Talkative, generous, and entirely unable to keep a secret for longer than a day.

## Appearance

Broad, sunburnt, missing the tip of his left ear.

## Goals

To be relieved of the post before winter.
