## Biography

Elara Voss has kept the archive at Thornwick for nineteen years. She came north
after the flooding of the lower library at Ashmere, carrying what she could save
in two oilcloth satchels.

## Personality

Precise, dry, and slow to trust. She answers questions with questions when she
suspects the asker has not earned the answer.

## Appearance

Tall, grey-streaked hair kept in a single braid. Ink-stained fingers.

## Goals

To finish the index before her eyes fail her.
