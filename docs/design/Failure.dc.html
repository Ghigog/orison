Three rules
A failure is never a toast. Toasts expire; the missing turn does not.
The character never apologises on the engine’s behalf. No line is invented to cover a call that did not happen.
Every kind that is reportable has one primary action. Cancelled is the one that is not reportable, and draws nothing.
