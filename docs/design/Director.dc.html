The Director strip
One row at the top of the dialogue panel, directly above the nameplate, so it sits in the reading path rather than off in a status bar. It renders DirectorState and nothing else. Idle draws nothing at all.
Idle no beat in flight, none queued
The strip collapses. A row that says “idle” is a row that teaches the player to stop reading it.
Researching retrieval pre-pass, and any research calls the profile keeps
Bram is remembering. The Mill Road the miller and his debt researching
Chips are the notes retrieval actually returned. They are the player’s own vault, named back to them.
Composing the Director model call is in flight
Bram is thinking about what happens next. composing
No chips: there is nothing honest to name yet. The absence is the difference between the two states.
Ready a beat is composed and waiting for a quiet moment
Bram has decided something. ready
Solar Flare is the only accent the strip ever uses, and only here. It is the one state that is about to change the scene.
Rules the strip has to obey
It never blocks anything. Nothing in the window waits on the Director.
It never shows a percentage or an estimate. It has neither, and inventing one is the apology the product cannot afford.
It names the character, not the system. “Bram is thinking” is anticipation; “Director: composing” is a progress bar with extra steps.
The lowercase state name on the right is a debug affordance, and belongs behind the developer setting.
